Current work

Research areas

01

Regional food systems

Food security, price forecasting, and markets for regional food supply chains.

  • Food Insecurity, Neighborhood Food Context and Cardiometabolic and Behavioral Health Risk in Arkansas

    I am a Co-Principal Investigator on this food security project, carried out with the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS). It is supported by a 2026–27 Watershed Grant from the Institute for Integrative and Innovative Research (I³R) at the University of Arkansas.

    • I³R Watershed Grant
    • Co-PI
    • With UAMS
    • $100,000

  • CultivateIQ: agribusiness price forecasting and decision support

    I am the product developer and technology transfer lead for CultivateIQ, an agribusiness price forecasting and decision support platform for small and midsize farmers and food distributors. It is being developed at the University of Arkansas I³R under the NSF Convergence Accelerator program, with price forecasting built on AI pipelines.

    • NSF Convergence Accelerator
    • Product developer and technology transfer lead
    • Entrepreneurship
    • Project spotlight

  • ProductionIQ: farm data validation platform

    Product developer and technology transfer lead for the ProductionIQ farm data validation platform, an NSF Convergence Accelerator project at the University of Arkansas I³R. ProductionIQ was selected for gBETA Arkansas, gener8tor’s seven-week accelerator for early-stage Arkansas startups, and is a collaboration with engineering at the University of Arkansas.

    • gBETA Arkansas
    • NSF Convergence Accelerator

  • NAFSI Hub Student Data Challenge

    Developed and launched the Student Data Challenge of the National Food Security & Innovation (NAFSI) Hub, in partnership with USDA. Student teams use the hub’s data and tools to work on real-world food security problems. The CultivateIQ challenge, “NAFSI Track 3: Social Media Price Signals for Fresh Produce Market,” ran from October 23 to November 2, 2026, and was open to undergraduate and graduate students at U.S. institutions. I also serve as data steward for the NAFSI Hub.

    • NAFSI Hub
    • In partnership with USDA
    • View the challenge
    • nafsihub.org

  • Food Safety Outbreaks, Market Responses, and Commodity Risk Profiles in U.S. Produce Markets

    A food safety paper with Meredith Adkins, Kristen Gibson, and Comfort K. Kwamikorkor, presented at the 17th Conference of Food Engineering (CoFE 2026) in November 2026.

    • Paper in progress
    • Presentations

  • Quantifying local food manufacturing business gaps

    A collaboration with USDA-AMS on the drivers of, and impediments to, small agri-food businesses, covering small business establishments in the Great Plains region.

    • USDA-AMS collaboration
