Postdoctoral Fellow · I³R, University of Arkansas

Economics for food, farms, and the networks that connect them.

I’m Priyanka Sharma, an agricultural economist (Ph.D., MBA) working on regional food systems, resource economics, and health economics. I also develop AI products grounded in economics, built on agentic architectures.

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Institute for Integrative and Innovative Research (I³R), University of Arkansas, where I am product developer and technology transfer lead for CultivateIQ, an agribusiness price forecasting platform. I am also a Co-Principal Investigator on an I³R Watershed Grant on food security with UAMS, and I developed and launched the NAFSI Hub Student Data Challenge in partnership with USDA.

Background

From industry to economics

Before turning to economics research, I worked in industry at Accenture, Denali – WNS, British Telecom, Flipkart (Walmart India), and Airtel, in software engineering and in technology sourcing and procurement.

I hold a Ph.D. in Agricultural and Applied Economics from Kansas State University (2025), an MBA in Supply Chain Management with a certification in Decision Analytics from North Carolina State University, and a B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication from Punjab Technical University.

That background in telecom, supply chains, and analytics carries into my research on regional food systems and rural infrastructure, and into my work on technology transfer and commercialization.
