Industry experience

Before turning to economics research, I worked at Accenture, Denali – WNS, British Telecom, Flipkart (Walmart India), and Airtel, in software engineering and in technology sourcing and procurement. I now work on product development and technology transfer at the University of Arkansas I³R.

Product development and technology transfer

University of Arkansas I³R, 2025 to present.

  • Product developer and technology transfer lead, CultivateIQ

    CultivateIQ is an agribusiness price forecasting and decision support platform for small and midsize farmers and food distributors, developed under the NSF Convergence Accelerator program.

    • Drive the transition from academic research prototype to commercially viable product, integrating demand forecasting, supplier behavior modeling, and regional market analytics
    • Coordinate between technical development teams, data scientists, and agribusiness end-users to ensure platform scalability, interpretability, and adoption readiness
    • Develop go-to-market strategy, licensing frameworks, and stakeholder engagement plans for commercialization
    • NSF Convergence Accelerator
