Research experience

  1. Postdoctoral Research Scientist III, Agribusiness Techno-Economics Researcher

    Institute for Integrative and Innovative Research (I³R), University of Arkansas

    I³R is a translational research institute taking a convergence approach to commercializing new discoveries. Part of a project under an NSF Convergence Accelerator grant, developing the CultivateIQ platform, a decision support price analytics platform for food distributors, now entering its commercialization phase with a minimum viable product.

    Platform design and economic translation

    • Contributed to CultivateIQ design by translating agribusiness decision problems into formal technical requirements while supporting development of modules linking demand forecasting, supplier behavior, and regional market conditions
    • Integrated econometric reasoning, market structure information flow, and cost–revenue logic into platform workflows and analytics modules

    Commercialization and stakeholder alignment

    • Acted as product manager bridging technical teams, data scientists, program managers, and end users during the commercialization phase
    • Evaluated trade-offs across data sources, modeling approaches, and system architecture to ensure scalability, interpretability, and user adoption, ensuring alignment with agribusiness practices, policy context, and extension-style usability for non-technical stakeholders
    • Supported the transition from research prototype to commercialization-ready platform, emphasizing decision-ready outputs over purely technical metrics
    • Filed 1 IP disclosure, with additional disclosures in preparation for agribusiness forecasting innovations

    2025 – Present
